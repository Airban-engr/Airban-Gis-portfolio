Land Surveying Services in Ghana

Looking for a reliable land surveyor in Ghana? Airban Engineering helps clients verify land boundaries, prepare cadastral plans, carry out topographic surveys and support land documentation processes.

Whether you are buying land, preparing for construction, registering property or resolving boundary uncertainty, a professional survey helps you make informed decisions and avoid costly mistakes.

Our Main Surveying Services

  • Boundary Surveys: Confirming and marking accurate land boundaries.
  • Cadastral Surveys: Supporting land registration and official documentation.
  • Site Plans: Preparing clear plans for land and building-related processes.
  • Topographic Surveys: Capturing levels, contours and site features for design.
  • GIS & Mapping: Creating maps and spatial insights for better decisions.

Why Land Surveying Matters

Many land problems begin when boundaries are unclear. A proper survey reduces the risk of encroachment, disputes, wrong plot sizes and documentation delays.
