Why Most Land Disputes in Ghana Start With One Mistake

Confusion starts where boundaries are unclear. And in Ghana, this mistake has cost people land, money, and peace of mind.

The reality most land buyers ignore

Many people buy land based on trust, word of mouth, or physical inspection alone. Someone points and says: “This is your land.”

But without proper survey verification, what you’re buying is not land — it’s a story.

What happens when boundaries are not verified?

  • Land disputes with neighbors
  • Two people claiming the same land
  • Encroachment issues after construction
  • Delays at Lands Commission
  • Loss of money and legal stress

What a proper survey actually does

A professional land survey removes guesswork completely. It defines your land using coordinates, measurements, and verified boundaries.

  • Confirms exact boundary lines
  • Detects encroachment early
  • Supports land registration
  • Prevents future disputes

Before you buy, build, or invest — verify your land

A simple verification today can save you years of stress and financial loss.
